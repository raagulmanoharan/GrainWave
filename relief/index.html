Your instrument.

Camera

Move your hands or watch the world move. Gentle movement whispers; stronger movement builds a crescendo. Hold still to loop your melody softly. Each movement advances one note in a 16-note phrase. Start high or low to transpose the next phrase; movement sets expression.

Harmony

A composed 16-note minor-pentatonic phrase. Position shifts the phrase at its boundary. Motion supplies the triggers.

Tone

PULSE / NOISE

Movement clocks a fresh 16-note phrase over a four-bar breakbeat. The melody stays fixed until you change scenes or restart. After five still seconds, it loops automatically. A quiet sixteenth-note pulse always runs. Sustained activity builds the break, ghost hits, harmonic echoes and rising sweeps; stillness lets them recede. Echo and reverb affect the melodic voices, keeping drums tight. Dotted markers indicate looping; bright white marks the active notes.
