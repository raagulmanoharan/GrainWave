Sound & vision

Your instrument.

Camera

Move your hands or watch the world move. Gentle movement whispers; stronger movement builds a crescendo. Hold still to let it fade. Movement at the top plays high notes. Movement at the bottom plays low notes. Your position sets pitch; your movement sets expression.

Harmony

Six notes, one octave. Vertical motion chooses pitch; energy opens the beat. A repeating motif keeps the phrase connected.

Tone

PULSE / NOISE

Fast, minimal two-bar breaks. Short noise resonators, clipped bass and a recurring six-note pentatonic motif. Movement triggers an immediate tuned pulse. Stillness stops the rhythm. Echo repeats both the sound and the moving image, leaving fading traces at half-beat intervals.
