Sound & vision

Your instrument.

Camera

Move your hands or watch the world move. Gentle movement whispers; stronger movement builds a crescendo. Hold still to let it fade. Movement at the top plays high notes. Movement at the bottom plays low notes. Your position sets pitch; your movement sets expression.

Harmony

Every measured movement updates the instrument immediately. Vertical position sets the lead note. Soft consonant harmonies support it; the rhythmic echo repeats what you play. The visual layer never selects or changes pitches.

Tone

AIR / CIRCUIT

Motion opens a four-bar arrangement: warm seventh chords, bass, soft broken beats and a repeating bell motif. Your vertical position still plays the lead immediately. Stillness lets the piece fade.
