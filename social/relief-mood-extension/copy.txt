RELIEF / SHORT COPY FOR THE OPEN SPACES

01 / Beside the birds
I saw a reel where birds crossing a playhead became notes. I wanted to try it with the camera already in my pocket.

02 / Beside the hand
No keys to learn. Hold the camera steady and let something move through the frame. The movement decides when the next note plays.

03 / Beside the traffic
At first, cars went unnoticed and a still table made notes. Getting the camera to notice the right things took a few attempts.

04 / Beside the texture
Dithering began as a way to soften the rough edges of the prototype. Then the grain became part of Relief itself.

05 / Beside the floral earphones
Headphones recommended. The sound is quieter than the image: a melody, a little noise, echoes that hang around.

06 / Beside the beach viewfinder
Same scene. Another way to see it. The image becomes grain; movement becomes a reason to play the next note.

07 / Beside the new mood images
Leaves, water, a passing bicycle. You don't need a special scene. Something small moving is enough.

08 / Near the end
KEEP saves the recent notes and the spaces between them. Let that moment repeat while you find another. Up to four can play together.

09 / Closing
make break scrap, have fun
